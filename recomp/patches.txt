# The game's logic rate (issue #12). fn_8003AEB0 runs every frame and derives the game's
# time step from the frames per second it is told (sda-30252: 60 in play, 30 in some
# cutscenes): the 16.16 time a frame adds to the game clock, the float time step and the
# frame multiplier that hundreds of sites read. Reading it divided by the step scale makes
# it 120 (or 60) at 120 fps, and leaves it as it is at 60, where the scale is 1.
8003AEC4 c->r[6] = (uint32_t)(int32_t)((double)(int32_t)LD32((c->r[13] + (uint32_t)-30252)) / gcn_step_scale(0x8003AEC4u, 1) + 0.5);
